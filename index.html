About

Brian T. Jaynes is a filmmaker, screenwriter, photographer and cinematographer based in East Texas. His work moves between live-action features, AI-assisted animation, and songwriting, with a slate of projects developed for faith-market distributors.

He grew up in Lindale, Texas, and is a single father of twin sons.
